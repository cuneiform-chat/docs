# Billing & Plans

Manage your subscription and understand your billing.

## Overview

Cuneiform Chat offers flexible plans to match your needs:
- **Trial** — A free plan that renews every month and never expires
- **Starter** — For teams getting started
- **Plus** — For growing businesses
- **Enterprise** — For large-scale deployments

## Billing Dashboard

Access billing from the **Billing** menu in the sidebar:

- Current plan and status
- Usage this period
- Transaction history (Payments tab)
- Extra usage settings

## Quick Actions

| Action | Location |
|--------|----------|

| Add extra usage funds | Extra Usage section |

## Billing Cycle

- Plans are billed on the cadence you chose at checkout — the [pricing page](https://cuneiform.chat/pricing) lists which cadences are currently offered and what each costs
- Credits are re-granted on their own cycle of roughly 30 days, whatever your billing cadence
- Upgrades take effect immediately
- Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing term, and plan changes are locked until then — on a term longer than a month, that wait is correspondingly longer

## Payment Methods

Accepted payment methods:
- Credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex)
- Debit cards
