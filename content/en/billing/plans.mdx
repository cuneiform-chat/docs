# Plans & Pricing

Choose the right plan for your needs.

## Available Plans

### Trial

Start free and stay free — the Trial plan has no end date.

**Includes:**

- Full feature access
- A monthly credit allowance, re-granted every credit period (about every 30 days)
- No credit card required

**Monthly renewal:** The Billing page shows when your credits renew. Your allowance refreshes at the start of every monthly period, for as long as you use Cuneiform Chat — there is no expiry and no limit on the number of periods.

**Best for:** Evaluating Cuneiform Chat before committing — and staying on a free plan for as long as it covers your usage.

---

### Starter

For teams ready to get started with AI chatbots.

**Includes:**

- Core features and integrations
- Standard monthly credits
- Standard support

**Best for:** Small teams deploying their first chatbot.

---

### Plus

For businesses ready to deploy AI chatbots.

**Includes:**

- Generous monthly credits
- All integrations (Telegram, Discord, Slack, WhatsApp, Google Chat, Meta)
- Priority support
- Advanced analytics

**Best for:** Small to medium businesses with active customer support needs.

---

### Enterprise

For organizations with high-volume requirements.

**Includes:**

- Higher credit allocation
- Dedicated support
- Volume discounts

**Best for:** Large organizations with significant chatbot usage.

## Credit System

Cuneiform Chat uses a credit-based system:

- Credits are consumed when your agents answer questions
- Document processing uses credits
- Credit allocation depends on your plan

See [Usage & Credits](/billing/usage) for details on how credits work.

## Choosing a Plan

### Trial → Starter

Upgrade when you:

- Have tested and validated your use case
- Are ready to deploy to users
- Need more credits than trial provides

### Starter → Plus

Consider Plus when you:

- Need more seats for a growing team
- Require additional agents and storage
- Want higher monthly credit allocation

### Plus → Enterprise

Consider Enterprise when you:

- Approach Plus plan limits regularly
- Require dedicated support
- Have specific compliance requirements

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I switch plans anytime?

Yes! Upgrades take effect immediately with prorated charges. Downgrades are scheduled for the end of your current billing term. Once a downgrade is scheduled, no further plan changes can be made until that term ends — on a term longer than a month, that wait is correspondingly longer.

### What happens if I exceed my credits?

You'll receive warnings as you approach your limit. If extra usage is enabled, you can continue using the platform. If not, some features may be restricted until the next billing period.

### Is there a free tier?

Yes. The Trial plan is free, never expires, and re-grants its credit allowance at the start of every monthly period. Upgrade to a paid plan when you need more credits, more seats or more agents than it provides.

### Do unused credits roll over?

Credits reset at the start of each credit period — roughly every 30 days, on every billing cadence. Your monthly allocation refreshes when that new credit period begins, which on an annual plan happens about twelve times per charge.

### Is website crawling included in all plans?

Yes. All plans include website crawling. The number of pages you can crawl per session depends on your plan. See [Website Crawling](/knowledge-base/website-crawling) for details.

### Which plans include Human Handoff?

Human Handoff is available on all plans, including Trial. It can be enabled per agent in the agent configuration. See [Configuration Options](/agents/configuring#human-handoff) for setup details.
