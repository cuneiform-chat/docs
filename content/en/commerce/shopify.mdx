# Shopify

**Install once from the Shopify App Store, link it to your workspace, and your agents are answering from your store's catalog, orders, and inventory.** No API keys to generate, no redirect URLs to configure, no plugin to install.

import { Callout } from 'nextra/components'

<Callout type="info">
  Shopify works on every plan, including the free trial. Connecting a store costs nothing extra.
</Callout>

## What you get

Once your store is linked, your store agent can:

- **Answer catalog questions** from your real products, variants, options, and prices
- **Read stock and price from your synced catalog**, kept current by webhooks so it isn't offering what you just sold out of
- **Recommend in-stock products** rather than whatever it remembers
- **Look up orders** for shoppers who can prove the order is theirs
- **Send order-status updates** to shoppers who opt in, in the same chat
- **Hand a ready-to-buy shopper to your Shopify checkout** with their whole cart intact

## Prerequisites

- A **Shopify store** you administer
- A Cuneiform Chat workspace, and an **Owner or Admin** account on it — linking a store is an admin action
- At least one agent, or room in your plan to create one (see [Your store agent](/commerce#your-store-agent))

## How connecting works

Connecting Shopify is a two-part handshake: Shopify installs the app, then **you** link that installed app to your Cuneiform Chat workspace. Shopify knows which store you are; only you know which workspace it belongs to.

### 1. Install from the Shopify App Store

Find **Cuneiform Chat - AI Commerce** in the Shopify App Store and install it. Shopify handles the installation itself and asks you to approve the permissions below. Nothing on our side is involved yet.

### 2. Open the app in your Shopify admin

The app opens **inside `admin.shopify.com`** as an embedded app. This is your Shopify-side home for the integration — setup, connection status, configuration, a dashboard, and billing all live here.

### 3. Click "Link your workspace"

Until the store is linked, the embedded app shows a **"Link your workspace"** banner. Clicking it opens Cuneiform Chat in a **new top-level browser tab** rather than inside the Shopify frame. That is deliberate: signing in cannot happen inside another site's iframe, so the linking step has to break out.

### 4. Approve the link

You land on the claim page in Cuneiform Chat with your store already identified. Confirm, and the store is attached to your workspace. You're redirected to **Commerce**, and the initial catalog sync starts.

What you can see on that page:

| What you see | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Sign-in prompt** | You weren't signed in. Sign in and the link picks up where it left off. |
| **Confirm the link** | You're an Owner or Admin — this is the normal path. |
| **Ask an admin to finish this** | You're a Member. Members can't connect integrations; send the link to an Owner or Admin. |
| **Store linked** | Done. The catalog sync is already running. |
| **This connection link expired** | The linking ticket is stale, already used, or no longer recognized. Go back to the app in your Shopify admin and click **Link your workspace** again. |
| **This store is connected elsewhere** | The store is already linked to a different workspace. Disconnect it there first. |
| **This workspace already has a store** | One Shopify store per workspace. Disconnect the current one before linking a new one. |
| **Couldn't connect your store** | Something went wrong on our side. A **Try again** button retries in place — you don't need to start over from Shopify. |
| **We couldn't complete the connection** | The link failed before it reached us. Re-open the app in your Shopify admin and start the link again. |

## Permissions we ask for

Cuneiform Chat requests **five read-only permissions**. There is no write permission in the set — the app cannot change anything in your store.

| Permission | Why we need it |
|---|---|
| `read_products` | Read your catalog: products, variants, options, prices, images |
| `read_inventory` | Read stock levels so the agent only recommends what's available |
| `read_orders` | Look up a shopper's order and send them status updates |
| `read_fulfillments` | Report shipping progress on those orders |
| `read_customers` | Match a shopper's phone number against the order they're asking about |

<Callout type="info">
  **Read-only means read-only.** The app cannot create, edit, or delete products; cannot adjust inventory; cannot create, edit, or cancel orders; and cannot capture payments. Every purchase completes on your own Shopify checkout.
</Callout>

## One store per workspace

A workspace connects **one Shopify store**. It can also connect one WooCommerce store at the same time — see [Commerce](/commerce#you-can-connect-both-at-once).

If someone tries to link a store that's already connected to a different workspace, the attempt is refused rather than silently taking it over.

## There is no "Reconnect" button

Shopify has no reconnect step, and you should never go looking for one. Permission changes ride the app itself: when the app's permission set changes, Shopify prompts you to approve the update the next time you open it. There is nothing to re-authorize by hand.

<Callout type="warning">
  This is the main way the Shopify and WooCommerce flows differ. WooCommerce **does** have a **Reconnect** button, because a self-hosted store's API keys can be revoked from inside the store. Shopify's cannot.
</Callout>

## After you connect

- **Your catalog starts syncing immediately.** See [Catalog & Knowledge](/commerce/catalog-and-knowledge) for what the sync covers and how it stays current.
- **One agent becomes your store agent**, chosen for you at connect. See [Your store agent](/commerce#your-store-agent).
- **Order-status updates are on by default**, and the only gate belongs to the shopper: they opt in after verifying their order. See [Orders](/commerce/orders#order-status-updates).

## Troubleshooting

### The "Link your workspace" banner won't go away

The link hasn't completed. Click it again — it opens a fresh linking ticket in a new tab. If it opens and immediately says the link expired, the ticket sat unused too long; just start it again.

### The link opened but nothing happened

The linking step must open in a **top-level tab**, not inside the Shopify frame. If your browser blocked the new tab, allow pop-ups for `admin.shopify.com` and try again.

### "This store is connected elsewhere"

The store is linked to another Cuneiform Chat workspace — often a trial workspace created earlier. Sign in to that workspace, disconnect the store there, then link it here.

### A Member can't finish the link

Linking a store requires the **Integration connect** permission, which Owners and Admins have and Members don't. Forward the link to an Owner or Admin.

### The catalog is empty right after linking

The first import backfills the whole catalog and takes a few minutes on a large store. If it still looks empty afterwards, run **Re-sync now** from **Commerce → Shopify → Catalog & Data**. While the catalog is still empty, the checklist on the Overview tab also offers a one-click **Sync now**.

### The agent isn't answering product questions

Check that you're talking to the **store agent** — the one wired to the store at connect time. Its agent page shows a read-only panel saying it's managed by your store. Other agents don't carry the commerce tools.

## Disconnecting

Disconnect from **Commerce → Shopify → Disconnect**. Disconnecting:

- Removes the stored connection and the synced catalog
- Clears the product knowledge built from that catalog, so agents stop answering from it
- Resets the store agent's commerce grant, reverts its commerce persona if you never edited it, and **archives the agent itself** — reconnecting the same store reclaims it with its tuning and version history intact
- **Permanently deletes any Storefront scoped to that store**, along with its analytics history. Storefronts you built by hand are untouched
- Archives the commerce Agent Flows that came with the store
- Deletes the AI quiz suggestions outright, so a future reconnect starts from a clean slate

<Callout type="warning">
  If you still have a **WooCommerce** store connected, only the workspace-wide steps — archiving commerce Agent Flows and deleting quiz suggestions — are skipped. The Shopify store's own catalog, product knowledge, Storefronts, analytics and store agent are always torn down.
</Callout>

Uninstalling the app from your Shopify admin ends Shopify's side of the relationship as well.
