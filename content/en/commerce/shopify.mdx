# Shopify

**Connect your Shopify store once and your agents are answering from your catalog, orders, and inventory.** Setup takes about five minutes, happens entirely inside Cuneiform Chat, and needs no theme edits and no plugin.

import { Callout } from 'nextra/components'

<Callout type="info">
  Shopify works on every plan, including the free trial. Connecting a store costs nothing extra.
</Callout>

## What you get

Once your store is connected, your store agent can:

- **Answer catalog questions** from your real products, variants, options, and prices
- **Read stock and price from your synced catalog**, kept current by webhooks so it isn't offering what you just sold out of
- **Recommend in-stock products** rather than whatever it remembers
- **Look up orders** for shoppers who can prove the order is theirs
- **Send order-status updates** to shoppers who opt in, in the same chat
- **Hand a ready-to-buy shopper to your Shopify checkout** with their whole cart intact

## Prerequisites

- A **Shopify store** you administer
- A Cuneiform Chat workspace, and an **Owner or Admin** account on it — connecting a store is an admin action
- At least one agent, or room in your plan to create one (see [Your store agent](/commerce#your-store-agent))

<Callout type="warning">
  **Collaborator accounts can't complete setup.** Part of the setup happens in your Shopify admin's Dev Dashboard, which Shopify does not offer to collaborator accounts. If an agency manages your store, the store owner — or a staff account with app permissions — has to do that part.
</Callout>

## How connecting works

You create a small app inside your own Shopify admin, install it on your store, and paste its credentials into Cuneiform Chat. Your store's data never leaves your Shopify account by any other route, and the app belongs to you rather than to us.

**The step-by-step walkthrough lives in the product**, where it can show you the exact values to enter for your store — those values depend on your workspace and on the API version we're currently running, so a copy of them here would go stale without telling you.

To start: open **Commerce → Shopify** in Cuneiform Chat and follow the setup steps on the connect screen. It walks you through creating the app, requesting the permissions below, installing it, and pasting in the credentials.

<Callout type="info">
  **Installing the app is its own step.** In Shopify's Dev Dashboard, releasing a version of your app is not the same as installing it on your store. If you stop at release, the credentials are rejected when you paste them in — the connect screen says so explicitly when that happens.
</Callout>

## Permissions we ask for

Your app requests **five read-only permissions**. There is no write permission in the set — it cannot change anything in your store.

| Permission | Why we need it |
|---|---|
| `read_products` | Read your catalog: products, variants, options, prices, images |
| `read_inventory` | Read stock levels so the agent only recommends what's available |
| `read_orders` | Look up a shopper's order and send them status updates |
| `read_fulfillments` | Report shipping progress on those orders |
| `read_customers` | Match a shopper's phone number against the order they're asking about |

<Callout type="info">
  **Read-only means read-only.** The app cannot create, edit, or delete products; cannot adjust inventory; cannot create, edit, or cancel orders; and cannot capture payments. Every purchase completes on your own Shopify checkout.
</Callout>

### If a permission isn't granted [#if-a-permission-isnt-granted]

Shopify restricts customer and order access on its cheapest plans, so a store on Basic or Starter may not be able to grant `read_orders` or `read_customers`. When that happens the connection still succeeds and Cuneiform Chat tells you which permissions are missing, on the connect screen and again on **Commerce → Shopify → Connection** for as long as the gap is real.

Anything that depends on a missing permission stays off rather than failing quietly — order lookup and order-status updates are the usual casualties. Once your store's plan covers them, disconnect and reconnect the store so the new permissions are picked up.

## Opening Cuneiform Chat inside your Shopify admin

Optionally, your app can be marked **embedded**, which makes your Cuneiform Chat dashboard open inside `admin.shopify.com` alongside the rest of your store admin — setup, connection status, configuration, and a dashboard. The connect screen gives you the two values Shopify asks for. Skipping this changes nothing about how the integration works; it only changes where you open it from.

## One store per workspace

A workspace connects **one Shopify store**. It can also connect one WooCommerce store at the same time — see [Commerce](/commerce#you-can-connect-both-at-once).

If you try to connect a store that's already connected to a different workspace, the attempt is refused rather than silently taking it over.

## Changing permissions or credentials later

There is no separate "Reconnect" button. Your app's permissions and credentials are recorded when you connect and aren't rewritten afterwards, so any change you make in Shopify — adding a scope, rotating the client secret — reaches Cuneiform Chat by **disconnecting and reconnecting the store**.

<Callout type="warning">
  Reconnecting re-runs the teardown described under [Disconnecting](#disconnecting), so read that first if you have Storefronts scoped to the store.
</Callout>

## After you connect

- **Your catalog starts syncing immediately.** See [Catalog & Knowledge](/commerce/catalog-and-knowledge) for what the sync covers and how it stays current.
- **One agent becomes your store agent**, chosen for you at connect. See [Your store agent](/commerce#your-store-agent).
- **Order-status updates are on by default**, and the only gate belongs to the shopper: they opt in after verifying their order. See [Orders](/commerce/orders#order-status-updates).

## Troubleshooting

### The credentials were rejected

The most common cause is that the app was released but never **installed** on the store — those are two separate actions in Shopify's Dev Dashboard. The connect screen distinguishes that case from a genuine credential mismatch, so read the message it gives you rather than assuming the Client ID and Secret are wrong.

### Order lookup and shipping updates aren't available

Check **Commerce → Shopify → Connection** for a missing-permission notice. On Shopify's cheapest plans `read_orders` and `read_customers` may not be grantable at all — see [If a permission isn't granted](#if-a-permission-isnt-granted).

### The catalog is empty right after connecting

The first import backfills the whole catalog and takes a few minutes on a large store. If it still looks empty afterwards, run **Re-sync now** from **Commerce → Shopify → Catalog & Data**. While the catalog is still empty, the checklist on the Overview tab also offers a one-click **Sync now**.

### The agent isn't answering product questions

Check that you're talking to the **store agent** — the one wired to the store at connect time. Its agent page shows a read-only panel saying it's managed by your store. Other agents don't carry the commerce tools.

### A Member can't connect the store

Connecting a store requires the **Integration connect** permission, which Owners and Admins have and Members don't.

## Disconnecting [#disconnecting]

Disconnect from **Commerce → Shopify → Disconnect**. Disconnecting:

- Removes the stored connection and the synced catalog
- Clears the product knowledge built from that catalog, so agents stop answering from it
- Resets the store agent's commerce grant, reverts its commerce persona if you never edited it, and **archives the agent itself** — reconnecting the same store reclaims it with its tuning and version history intact
- **Permanently deletes any Storefront scoped to that store**, along with its analytics history. Storefronts you built by hand are untouched
- Archives the commerce Agent Forms that came with the store
- Deletes the AI quiz suggestions outright, so a future reconnect starts from a clean slate

<Callout type="warning">
  If you still have a **WooCommerce** store connected, only the workspace-wide steps — archiving commerce Agent Forms and deleting quiz suggestions — are skipped. The Shopify store's own catalog, product knowledge, Storefronts, analytics and store agent are always torn down.
</Callout>

**Your app stays installed in your Shopify admin.** Disconnecting here removes our side of the relationship, not the app you created — it belongs to your store. Delete it from your Shopify admin if you want it gone.
