# WooCommerce Integration

Connect your self-hosted **WooCommerce** store so your AI agent can answer product questions, look up a shopper's order, recommend in-stock items, and send order-status updates — all from your real, live catalog and order data.

## Overview

The WooCommerce integration links your self-hosted store to your AI agents over the WooCommerce REST API. WooCommerce is your commerce backend; your Cuneiform agent is the conversation layer across every channel you deploy it to. Once connected, the agent can:

- **Catalog Q&A** — answer from your real products, variants, prices, and current availability
- **Order lookup (WISMO)** — let shoppers check "where is my order?" with their order number plus the email or phone on the order
- **Recommendations** — suggest products that are actually **in stock**
- **Order-status updates** — message shoppers who opt in with shipping updates in the same chat
- **Live inventory** — always read prices and stock fresh at answer time
- **Checkout handoff** — send a ready-to-buy shopper to your own WooCommerce checkout

## Prerequisites

- A **self-hosted WooCommerce store** you administer
- Your **store URL** (for example, `https://your-store.com`)
- At least **one agent** to become your commerce agent (you can create one during the connect flow)

You do **not** need to install a plugin or write any code. The standard connect flow is a one-time approval; a read-only consumer key/secret is only needed if your store sits behind a firewall.

## Setup Steps

### 1. Open Commerce

In the admin panel, go to **Commerce** and choose **WooCommerce**. Commerce is the single home for the integration — the connect card, your Product Library, Settings, and Analytics all live here.

### 2. Connect your store

Enter your store URL and click **Connect**. You'll approve a one-time WooCommerce REST API authorization on your store, then return to Commerce. If your store sits behind a firewall that blocks the redirect, expand **Enter API keys manually** and paste a **read-only** consumer key and secret (generated in WooCommerce → Settings → Advanced → REST API).

### 3. Choose your commerce agent

Commerce is **opt-in per agent** and **off by default**. At connect time, pick the **one** agent that should become your commerce agent — an existing active agent or a new one. Only that agent receives the WooCommerce tools; your other agents are unaffected. You can change this later under **Commerce → Settings**.

### 4. Let your catalog sync

Connecting kicks off a one-time import of your products, variants, prices, images, and stock. After that, changes sync automatically, and a daily reconcile catches anything missed.

### 5. Try it

Open your commerce agent and ask a catalog question ("what sizes does this come in?", "is the blue one in stock?") or look up an order ("where is my order #1001?"). The agent answers from your live WooCommerce data.

## How It Works

```
Shopper asks a question
  → Commerce agent reads your live WooCommerce catalog & orders (read-only)
  → Answers about products, stock, and order status
  → Hands off to your own WooCommerce checkout to buy
```

The agent reads your products, prices, inventory, and orders directly through the WooCommerce REST API and keeps prices and stock fresh — no copy-pasting your catalog into a knowledge base.

## Features

### Read-only by design

The integration only **reads** from WooCommerce (products, inventory, orders). It never changes your catalog or inventory, never captures payments, and never places orders. Checkout always happens on your own WooCommerce store.

### One store, one commerce agent

You connect **one WooCommerce store per organization** and grant commerce to **exactly one agent** at connect time. Other agents stay non-commerce unless you turn them on under **Commerce → Settings**.

### Verified order lookup

Shoppers see order details only after providing the exact order number **and** the email or phone on the order. A wrong contact and a non-existent order return the same "couldn't find it" answer with no details — anti-enumeration hardening that protects your customers.

### Live catalog & inventory

The agent reads prices and stock fresh at answer time, so recommendations are limited to items that are actually in stock. A daily reconcile repairs anything a sync misses.

### Opt-in order-status updates

After a shopper verifies their order in chat, they can opt in to receive shipping updates on a supported channel. Updates only fire when you've enabled them in **Commerce → Settings → Notifications** and the shopper has opted in.

## Security

- **Read-only access** — the integration cannot create, edit, or delete anything in your store, change inventory, or capture payments
- **Tenant isolation** — your catalog and orders are scoped to your organization and never visible to other tenants
- **Verified order lookup** — order details require the exact order number plus the matching email or phone (anti-enumeration)
- **Opt-in notifications** — shipping updates require an explicit per-shopper opt-in in chat
- **Secure checkout** — purchases complete on your own WooCommerce checkout; no card or payment data passes through the agent

## Troubleshooting

### Connecting hangs or fails

Connecting needs the server to reach your store URL and — for the standard flow — needs you to complete the one-time authorization on your store. If it stalls, it's usually a network or firewall issue reaching the store, not your credentials. If your store is firewalled, connect with a **read-only** consumer key/secret instead (**Enter API keys manually**).

### The agent won't answer product questions

Commerce is **off by default** and granted to one agent at connect. If a particular agent isn't answering catalog questions, open **Commerce → Settings** and turn on its commerce toggle.

### The catalog looks empty right after connecting

The first import backfills your whole catalog and can take a few minutes for a large store. Once it finishes, your products are searchable; after that, changes sync automatically.

### A price or stock level looks out of date

The agent reads prices and stock fresh every time, so they shouldn't go stale. In the rare case a sync is missed, the daily reconcile repairs the catalog automatically.

### Order lookup can't find an order

The order-lookup tool is deliberately strict: it returns details only when the shopper provides the exact order number **and** an email or phone that matches the order.

### No order-status updates are sent

Updates require both gates: enable them in **Commerce → Settings → Notifications**, and have the shopper opt in in chat on a supported channel.

## FAQ

### Do I need a paid plan to connect WooCommerce?

No. The WooCommerce integration works on every plan, including the free trial. You can connect a store and use catalog Q&A, order lookup, recommendations, and order-status updates on any plan.

### Do I have to install a plugin?

No plugin. The standard flow is a one-time approval — enter your store URL and approve a read-only WooCommerce REST API authorization. You only paste a **read-only** consumer key/secret if your store's firewall blocks the automatic redirect.

### Can all of my agents use WooCommerce, or just one?

Commerce is opt-in per agent and off by default. At connect you grant commerce to **one** agent; only that agent gets the WooCommerce tools. You can enable commerce for more agents later under **Commerce → Settings**.

### What does the integration do with my store data?

It reads your catalog, inventory, and orders to answer questions and send opted-in updates. It is read-only — it never changes your store, never changes inventory, and never captures payments. Checkout always happens on your own WooCommerce store.

### Does the agent ever change my store or place orders?

No. The integration is strictly read-only. It never edits products, never adjusts inventory, and never places or pays for orders on a shopper's behalf — those actions always happen on your own WooCommerce store.
