# Getting Started

Get up and running with Cuneiform Chat in just a few minutes. This guide walks you through creating your first AI chatbot trained on your documents.

## What You'll Learn

- How to create and set up your account
- How to upload documents to your content
- How to create and configure your first agent
- How to test your agent in the playground

## Prerequisites

- A valid email address
- Documents you want your chatbot to learn from (PDFs, Word docs, text files, etc.)
