# Create an Account

Get started with Cuneiform Chat by creating your account.

## Sign Up

1. Go to [cuneiform.chat](https://cuneiform.chat)
2. Click **Get Started** or **Sign Up**
3. Choose your sign-in method:
   - **Google** — Click "Sign in with Google" for one-click access
   - **Microsoft** — Click "Sign in with Microsoft" to use your Microsoft 365 account
   - **Email** — Enter your email and click "Continue with Email"
4. If using email, check your inbox and click the magic link to sign in

## Sign In Options

You can sign in using:
- **Google** — One-click sign in with your Google account
- **Microsoft** — Sign in with your Microsoft 365 or work account
- **Email magic link** — Passwordless sign-in via a link sent to your email

## Your Workspace Is Ready

When you sign in for the first time, Cuneiform Chat sets up your workspace (organization) for you automatically — no setup form to fill in. We pick a sensible name from your email (for example, a company email becomes your company name), so you land straight in a ready-to-use workspace. Your workspace is where you manage agents, documents, and integrations.

You can rename your workspace and add details (industry, team size, support contact) anytime from **Account Management** in your profile menu.

Workspaces help you:

- Manage multiple agents under one account
- Invite team members and control access
- Track usage and billing across all your chatbots

### Welcome Screen

The first time you reach your dashboard, you'll see a one-time **welcome screen** that greets you by name and offers quick setup launchers for the most common first steps:

- Create your first agent
- Connect a channel (like Telegram or your website)
- Add knowledge for your agent to learn from
- Set your branding

Pick a launcher to get going, or click **Continue to dashboard** to skip ahead — you won't see the welcome screen again. The same setup launchers also stay available on your dashboard so you can return to them anytime.

## Trial Period

New accounts start with a free trial that includes:
- Full access to all features
- Credits to test document processing and AI queries
- No credit card required
