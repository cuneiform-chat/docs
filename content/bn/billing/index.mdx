# Billing & Plans

Manage your subscription and understand your billing.

## Overview

Cuneiform Chat offers flexible plans to match your needs:
- **Trial** — Try the platform free
- **Starter** — For teams getting started
- **Plus** — For growing businesses
- **Enterprise** — For large-scale deployments

## Billing Dashboard

Access billing from the **Billing & Plans** menu in the sidebar:

- Current plan and status
- Usage this period
- Transaction history
- Extra usage settings

## Quick Actions

| Action | Location |
|--------|----------|
| View current plan | Overview tab |
| Upgrade plan | Change Plan button |
| View transactions | Payments tab |
| Add extra usage funds | Extra Usage section |

## Billing Cycle

- Plans are billed monthly
- Usage resets at the start of each billing period
- Upgrades take effect immediately
- Downgrades take effect at the end of the billing period (plan changes locked until then)

## Payment Methods

Accepted payment methods:
- Credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex)
- Debit cards

## Questions?

For billing questions:
- Check the [Plans & Pricing](/billing/plans) page
- Review [Usage & Credits](/billing/usage) for understanding consumption
- Contact support for specific billing inquiries
