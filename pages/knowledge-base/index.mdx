# Knowledge Base

The knowledge base is where your AI agents find information to answer questions. It contains all the documents, files, and content your chatbots can access.

## How It Works

1. **Upload** — Add documents to your knowledge base
2. **Process** — Documents are automatically processed and indexed
3. **Search** — Agents search relevant content when answering questions
4. **Respond** — Answers are generated based on your actual content

## Key Concepts

### Documents

Individual files you upload. Each document is processed and made searchable.

### Chunks

Documents are split into smaller pieces called "chunks" for efficient searching. This allows agents to find the most relevant sections rather than entire documents.

### Embeddings

AI-generated representations of your content that enable semantic search. This means agents can find relevant information even when the exact words don't match.

## Best Practices

- **Keep content current** — Regularly update documents to maintain accuracy
- **Organize with folders** — Group related documents for easier management
- **Quality matters** — Well-structured documents produce better AI responses
- **Remove duplicates** — Avoid uploading the same content multiple times
