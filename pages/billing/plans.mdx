# Plans & Pricing

Choose the right plan for your needs.

## Available Plans

### Trial

Get started free and explore the platform.

**Includes:**

- Full feature access
- Limited credits for testing
- No credit card required
- 14-day trial period

**Trial Countdown:** The Billing page displays your remaining trial days. When your trial expires, upgrade to continue using the service.

**Best for:** Evaluating Cuneiform Chat before committing.

---

### Plus

For businesses ready to deploy AI chatbots.

**Includes:**

- Generous monthly credits
- All integrations (Telegram, Discord, Slack, WhatsApp, Google Chat, Meta)
- Priority support
- Advanced analytics

**Best for:** Small to medium businesses with active customer support needs.

---

### Enterprise

For organizations with high-volume requirements.

**Includes:**

- Higher credit allocation
- Dedicated support
- Volume discounts

**Best for:** Large organizations with significant chatbot usage.

## Credit System

Cuneiform Chat uses a credit-based system:

- Credits are consumed when your agents answer questions
- Document processing uses credits
- Credit allocation depends on your plan

See [Usage & Credits](/billing/usage) for details on how credits work.

## Choosing a Plan

### Trial → Plus

Upgrade when you:

- Have tested and validated your use case
- Are ready to deploy to users
- Need more credits than trial provides

### Plus → Enterprise

Consider Enterprise when you:

- Approach Plus plan limits regularly
- Require dedicated support
- Have specific compliance requirements

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I switch plans anytime?

Yes! Upgrade anytime and the change takes effect immediately. Downgrades take effect at your next billing period.

### What happens if I exceed my credits?

You'll receive warnings as you approach your limit. If extra usage is enabled, you can continue using the platform. If not, some features may be restricted until the next billing period.

### Is there a free tier?

The Trial plan is free and lets you fully evaluate the platform. For ongoing free usage, consider the Trial limitations and upgrade when you need more capacity.

### Do unused credits roll over?

Credits reset at the start of each billing period. Your monthly allocation refreshes when your new period begins.
