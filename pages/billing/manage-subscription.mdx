# Manage Subscription

Control your plan, payment methods, and billing settings.

## Accessing Subscription Settings

Go to **Billing & Plans** in the sidebar to manage your subscription.

## Upgrading Your Plan

### When to Upgrade

Consider upgrading when:
- Approaching your credit limits
- Needing features from a higher plan
- Growing your chatbot usage

### How to Upgrade

1. Go to **Billing** → **Change Plan**
2. Select your new plan
3. Confirm the change
4. New plan activates immediately

## Downgrading Your Plan

### Before You Downgrade

Review what changes:
- Credit allocation decreases
- Some features may become unavailable
- Verify current usage fits new plan

### How to Downgrade

1. Go to **Billing** → **Change Plan**
2. Select a lower plan
3. Confirm the change
4. Change takes effect at your next billing date

<Callout type="warning">
  Downgrades take effect at your next billing period. Ensure your usage will fit within the new plan's limits.
</Callout>

## Payment Methods

### Viewing Payment Methods

See your current payment method in **Billing** → **Payment Method**.

### Updating Payment Method

1. Go to **Billing** → **Payment Method**
2. Click **Update**
3. Enter new card details
4. Save changes

### Adding Backup Payment

Add a backup payment method to avoid service interruption if your primary method fails.

## Billing History

### View Invoices

Access past invoices:
1. Go to **Billing** → **History**
2. View or download any invoice
3. Invoices are available in PDF format

### Invoice Details

Each invoice shows:
- Billing period
- Plan charges
- Any additional charges
- Payment status

## Billing Information

### Update Billing Details

Update your billing information:
- Company name
- Billing address
- Tax ID (if applicable)

Changes apply to future invoices.

## Cancellation

### Before Canceling

Consider:
- Your agents will stop responding
- Documents remain stored for a limited time
- You can reactivate later

### How to Cancel

1. Go to **Billing** → **Cancel Subscription**
2. Complete the cancellation survey (optional)
3. Confirm cancellation
4. Access continues until the end of your billing period

### After Cancellation

- Service continues until period end
- Data retained per retention policy
- Reactivate anytime by subscribing again

## Reactivating

To reactivate a canceled subscription:

1. Log in to your account
2. Go to **Billing**
3. Select a plan
4. Complete payment

Your existing agents and documents (if retained) will become available again.

## Enterprise Billing

Enterprise plans have customized billing:
- Annual contracts available
- Custom payment terms
- Volume discounts
- Contact your account manager for changes

## FAQs

### When am I charged?

You're charged at the start of each billing period, based on your subscription date.

### Can I get a refund?

See our refund policy or contact support for specific situations.

### How do I update my billing email?

Update your billing email in **Settings** → **Account** → **Email Preferences**.

### What if my payment fails?

- You'll receive email notifications
- Service continues briefly with retry attempts
- Update payment method to avoid interruption
