# Usage & Credits

Understand how credits work and monitor your consumption.

## Credit System

Cuneiform Chat uses credits (called Platform Credits) to measure usage:
- **AI queries** — When your agents answer questions
- **Document processing** — When documents are uploaded and indexed
- **Embeddings** — Vector generation for search

## How Credits Are Used

### AI Queries

Each time your agent answers a question:
- Credits are consumed based on the AI model used
- Longer responses may use slightly more credits
- Failed queries don't consume credits

### Document Processing

When you upload documents:
- Credits are used to extract and process content
- Larger documents use more credits
- Re-processing uses additional credits

## Viewing Usage

Access your usage from **Billing & Plans** in the sidebar:

- **Current period usage** — Credits used this billing cycle
- **Remaining credits** — Available credits
- **Usage percentage** — Visual progress bar showing consumption

## Monitoring Usage

### Usage Display

The billing page shows:
- Credits used vs. allocated
- Usage percentage with visual progress bar
- Status indicator (normal, warning, exceeded, blocked)

### Usage Warnings

The platform automatically displays warnings as you approach your limits:

| Threshold | What Happens |
|-----------|--------------|
| ~80% used | Warning banner appears in Billing page |
| 100% used | "Limit Reached" banner with upgrade prompt |
| Blocked | Service paused until next period or upgrade |

These warnings appear automatically — no configuration needed.

## Optimizing Credit Usage

### Efficient Agents

- Write clear agent personas for accurate first responses

### Smart Document Management

- Upload only necessary documents
- Remove outdated content
- Avoid duplicate documents

### Usage Patterns

- Monitor which agents use the most credits
- Identify high-volume time periods
- Optimize based on patterns

## What Happens at Limits

### Approaching Limits

As you near your credit limit, warning banners appear in the Billing page prompting you to upgrade or enable extra usage.

### Exceeding Limits

When you exceed your monthly quota:
- A warning banner indicates "Limit Reached"
- Enable **Extra Usage** to continue without waiting
- Or upgrade your plan for more monthly credits

### Blocked State

If extra usage balance is also depleted:
- Service pauses until the next billing period
- Or add funds to your extra usage balance

## Extra Usage

Extra usage lets you continue using the service when your monthly quota runs out.

### Enabling Extra Usage

1. Go to **Billing & Plans**
2. Find the **Extra Usage** section
3. Toggle to enable
4. Complete the initial top-up (minimum $10)

### Managing Extra Usage

Once enabled, you can:

- **View balance** — See your available extra usage funds
- **Add funds** — Top up your balance ($10-$500 per transaction)
- **Set spending cap** — Limit monthly extra usage spending ($10-$500)

### How Extra Usage Works

- Charges are deducted from your extra usage balance
- Usage stops when you reach your spending cap
- Balance carries over between billing periods
- Cannot be disabled once enabled (use spending cap to limit)

<Callout type="info">
  Extra usage is only available for paid plans (Professional and Enterprise).
</Callout>

## Billing Period

- Credits reset at the start of each billing period
- Your billing date is shown in account settings
- Unused credits may not roll over (check your plan)
