# Usage & Credits

Understand how credits work and monitor your consumption.

## Credit System

Cuneiform Chat uses credits (called Platform Credits) to measure usage:
- **AI queries** — When your agents answer questions
- **Document processing** — When documents are uploaded and indexed
- **Embeddings** — Vector generation for search

## How Credits Are Used

### AI Queries

Each time your agent answers a question:
- Credits are consumed based on the AI model used
- Longer responses may use slightly more credits
- Failed queries don't consume credits

### Document Processing

When you upload documents:
- Credits are used to extract and process content
- Larger documents use more credits
- Re-processing uses additional credits

## Viewing Usage

Access your usage from **Settings** → **Billing** → **Usage**:

- **Current period usage** — Credits used this billing cycle
- **Remaining credits** — Available credits
- **Usage breakdown** — By category (queries, processing, etc.)

## Usage by Category

| Category | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| AI Queries | Agent responses to user questions |
| Document Processing | Uploading and indexing documents |
| Embeddings | Vector generation for search |

## Monitoring Usage

### Usage Dashboard

The dashboard shows:
- Real-time credit balance
- Daily consumption trends
- Projected usage for the period

### Usage Warnings

The platform automatically displays warnings as you approach your limits:

| Threshold | What Happens |
|-----------|--------------|
| ~80% used | Warning banner appears in dashboard |
| 100% used | "Limit Reached" banner with upgrade prompt |
| Blocked | Service paused until next period or upgrade |

These warnings appear automatically — no configuration needed.

## Optimizing Credit Usage

### Efficient Agents

- Write clear system prompts for accurate first responses
- Tune retrieval settings to reduce unnecessary searches
- Use appropriate response length settings

### Smart Document Management

- Upload only necessary documents
- Remove outdated content
- Avoid duplicate documents

### Usage Patterns

- Monitor which agents use the most credits
- Identify high-volume time periods
- Optimize based on patterns

## What Happens at Limits

### Approaching Limits

As you near your credit limit, warning banners appear in your dashboard prompting you to upgrade or enable extra usage.

### Exceeding Limits

Depending on your plan:
- Service may pause until the next period
- Overage charges may apply
- Upgrade to continue immediately

## Credit History

View historical usage:
- Previous billing periods
- Usage trends over time
- Breakdown by month

## Billing Period

- Credits reset at the start of each billing period
- Your billing date is shown in account settings
- Unused credits may not roll over (check your plan)
