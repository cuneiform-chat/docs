## Why Cuneiform Chat?

- **Document-Trained AI** — Your chatbot learns from PDFs, Word docs, presentations, websites, and more
- **Multiple Channels** — Deploy once, reach users on web, Telegram, Discord, Slack, and WhatsApp
- **No Code Required** — Create and configure agents through an intuitive dashboard
- **Accurate Responses** — RAG-powered retrieval ensures answers come from your actual content
- **Usage Analytics** — Track conversations, monitor costs, and understand user behavior

## Quick Start

1. [Create an account](/getting-started/create-account) on Cuneiform Chat
2. [Add your content](/getting-started/add-documents) to the knowledge base
3. [Create an agent](/getting-started/first-agent) and configure its behavior
4. [Test your agent](/getting-started/test-your-agent) in the playground
5. [Deploy](/integrations) to your preferred channels

## About the Name

*Cuneiform* was humanity's first writing system, developed in ancient Mesopotamia around 3400 BCE. Just as cuneiform tablets preserved knowledge for millennia, Cuneiform Chat helps you preserve and share your organization's knowledge through AI.

**Clay tablets to chatbot. Remembering the beginning.**
