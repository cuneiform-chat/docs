# Add Content

Your chatbot's knowledge comes from the content you provide. This guide shows you how to upload documents and crawl websites to build your content.

## Accessing Content

1. Sign in to your Cuneiform Chat dashboard
2. Navigate to **Knowledge** in the sidebar
3. Click **Upload Documents** or drag and drop files

## Supported File Types

Cuneiform Chat supports a wide range of document formats:

| Format | Extensions | Notes |
|--------|-----------|-------|
| PDF | `.pdf` | Text-based and scanned (OCR) |
| Word | `.docx`, `.doc` | Microsoft Word documents |
| Text | `.txt`, `.md`, `.rtf` | Plain text, Markdown, Rich Text |
| PowerPoint | `.pptx`, `.ppt` | Presentation slides |
| Web/Data | `.html`, `.htm`, `.json`, `.xml` | Web pages and structured data |

## Upload Methods

### Direct Upload

1. Click **Upload Documents**
2. Select files from your computer
3. Wait for processing to complete

### Drag and Drop

Simply drag files from your computer and drop them into the Content area.

### Cloud Storage

Connect your cloud storage for seamless imports:

- **Google Drive** — Import directly from your Drive
- **Dropbox** — Connect your Dropbox account
- **OneDrive** — Import from Microsoft OneDrive
- **Box** — Connect your Box account

See [Cloud Storage Integration](/knowledge-base/cloud-storage) for setup instructions.

### Website Crawling

Crawl public websites and import the content as documents:

1. Navigate to **Knowledge** and click the **Website** tab
2. Enter a URL and click **Crawl**
3. Review and edit crawled pages
4. Select pages and click **Process Selected**

The crawler follows links up to 2 levels deep. The number of pages you can crawl depends on your [plan](/billing/plans).

See [Website Crawling](/knowledge-base/website-crawling) for the full guide.

## Document Processing

After uploading or crawling, your content is automatically processed and made searchable by your agents.

## Best Practices

- **Quality over quantity** — Well-structured documents produce better responses
- **Keep content current** — Remove outdated documents to avoid confusion
- **Organize with folders** — Use the folder structure to keep documents organized
- **Crawl selectively** — Review and edit crawled pages before processing to remove navigation and boilerplate
