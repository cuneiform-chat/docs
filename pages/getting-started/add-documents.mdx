# Add Documents

Your chatbot's knowledge comes from the documents you provide. This guide shows you how to upload documents to your knowledge base.

## Accessing the Knowledge Base

1. Sign in to your Cuneiform Chat dashboard
2. Navigate to **Knowledge Base** in the sidebar
3. Click **Upload Documents** or drag and drop files

## Supported File Types

Cuneiform Chat supports a wide range of document formats:

| Format | Extensions | Notes |
|--------|-----------|-------|
| PDF | `.pdf` | Text-based and scanned (OCR) |
| Word | `.docx`, `.doc` | Microsoft Word documents |
| Text | `.txt`, `.md` | Plain text and Markdown |
| Spreadsheets | `.xlsx`, `.csv` | Excel and CSV files |
| PowerPoint | `.pptx` | Presentation slides |
| HTML | `.html` | Web pages |

## Upload Methods

### Direct Upload

1. Click **Upload Documents**
2. Select files from your computer
3. Wait for processing to complete

### Drag and Drop

Simply drag files from your computer and drop them into the Knowledge Base area.

### Cloud Storage

Connect your cloud storage for seamless imports:

- **Google Drive** — Import directly from your Drive
- **Dropbox** — Connect your Dropbox account
- **OneDrive** — Import from Microsoft OneDrive

See [Cloud Storage Integration](/knowledge-base/cloud-storage) for setup instructions.

## Document Processing

After upload, your documents go through several processing stages:

1. **Extraction** — Text is extracted from the document
2. **Chunking** — Content is split into searchable segments
3. **Embedding** — AI creates vector representations for semantic search
4. **Indexing** — Content becomes searchable by your agents

## Best Practices

- **Quality over quantity** — Well-structured documents produce better responses
- **Keep content current** — Remove outdated documents to avoid confusion
- **Organize with folders** — Use the folder structure to keep documents organized
- **Check for duplicates** — Avoid uploading the same content multiple times
